Advanced users only. Standard 5-field cron in UTC, for example 0 3 * * *.

Cleanup Rule

Only items marked played with Jellyfin's checkmark are eligible. Partial progress alone is skipped.

Users

Comma-separated Jellyfin user IDs. Leave empty to include all users.

Exclusions

Favorite movies are kept. Favorite episodes are kept. Favorite seasons keep their episodes. Favorite series keep all episodes.
Comma-separated words or path fragments, for example Kids, Concerts, /Archive/.
